Etiquette

Backgammon should be competitive, friendly and enjoyable for everyone. We ask players to follow a few simple guidelines when playing club and league matches.

  • Be respectful – Treat your opponent and other club members courteously, whether you win or lose.
  • Play at a reasonable pace – Take the time you need to make decisions, but avoid unnecessarily delaying the game.
  • Roll properly – Roll both dice together into the board and re-roll any cocked or invalid dice.
  • Move clearly – Make your moves clearly and avoid repeatedly moving checkers around while deciding what to play.
  • Handle mistakes fairly – If there is uncertainty over a move, score or rule, resolve it calmly. Ask another experienced club member if necessary.
  • Keep distractions reasonable – Avoid disrupting your opponent during a match and keep phone use to a minimum.
  • Respect the equipment – Treat club boards, dice, checkers and clocks with care.
  • Be a good sport – A handshake and a "good game" are encouraged, whatever the result.
  • Report results promptly – League and tournament results should be submitted to the club website after the match.

Match Rules

These rules apply to matches played as part of a League season or Knockout tournament.

  • Matches are played between two registered League Players.
  • Each competition uses a match length of first to 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 or 15 points.
  • A completed match must have a winner. The winning score must reach or exceed the selected match length, and the losing score must remain below it.
  • If the final game takes the winner beyond the selected match length, the full score should still be reported.
  • The result must include each player's score and the number of gammons and backgammons won by each player.
  • Either participating player may submit the initial result. Once submitted, a League Player cannot change it; corrections must be made by a League Admin.

League

Format and fixtures

  • Each League is identified by a division number and season number and has a published start and end date.
  • The League is played as a round robin. Every player meets every other player the same number of times.
  • A season may be configured for players to meet once, twice, three times or four times.
  • Fixtures are arranged in complete round-robin cycles, so every pairing is played once before the next cycle begins.
  • Players may be added during a season. When fixtures are updated, only the missing meetings are added; existing fixtures and results are retained.

Points and standings

  • A match win is worth 3 League points.
  • There are no drawn matches and no points are awarded for a draw.
  • During an active season, standings are ordered by total League points, then backgammons, then gammons.
  • When the season is completed, players tied on League points are separated first by head-to-head wins, then backgammons, then gammons.
  • Head-to-head compares wins only from matches played between the players tied on total League points.
  • If players remain equal after every applicable criterion, they share the same position. No additional tie-break is applied.

Completed seasons retain their players, fixtures, results, winner and runner-up as part of the club's historical record.

Knockouts

Draw and byes

  • Each Knockout tournament is identified by a season number and has a published start and end date.
  • The first-round draw is random.
  • When the number of entrants does not produce a complete bracket, the required first-round byes are assigned randomly.
  • A player receiving a first-round bye advances automatically. No match is recorded for the bye.
  • Once generated, the draw is retained and is not reshuffled.

Progress through the tournament

  • Each Knockout match must produce a winner; there are no drawn matches.
  • The winner of each match advances to the next round.
  • A later-round match is created once both advancing players are known.
  • The winner of the final is the Knockout champion.

Completed tournaments retain their entrants, bracket, matches and results as part of the club's historical record.

Rules Still To Be Agreed

The following situations are not yet covered by the competition rules and will be added once agreed by the club:

  • Deadlines for arranging and completing individual fixtures.
  • Postponed, unplayed or abandoned matches.
  • Player withdrawals, concessions and walkovers.
  • Result confirmation and the process for handling a disputed result.
  • Whether corrections may be made after a League or Knockout has been marked completed.
  • How to handle later Knockout rounds if an earlier result is corrected after the next match has already been played or created.
  • Rules governing clocks, the doubling cube, the Crawford rule, legal moves and other playing procedures.
